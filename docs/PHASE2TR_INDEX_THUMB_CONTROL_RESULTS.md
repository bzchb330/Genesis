# Phase 2T-R index+thumb control results

## Outcome

Phase 2T-R stopped before the common B distribution could be frozen. No eligible common B region: the validated native index+thumb region systematically overlaps the endpoint hand, while 3,568 positive-epsilon mapped-region candidates produced zero strict B-only successes.

This is not a formal FINGERTIP-versus-PALMAR result. No A+B calibration, matching, formal trials, McNemar test, or bootstrap was run, and transfer was not simulated.

## Targeted endpoints

- FINGERTIP_INDEX_THUMB_FREE: 100 valid / 328 attempts (0.3049); 82 local and 18 broader targeted states.
- PALMAR_INDEX_THUMB_FREE: 102 valid / 264 attempts (0.3864); 84 local and 18 broader targeted states.
- Every included state passed the exact identity assertion: occupied={middle, ring}, free={index, thumb}.

## Index+thumb B-only evidence

- Native Phase 2S proposal revalidation/search: 5/12 strict successes.
- Robustness: 25/100 (0.250).
- Middle/ring assistance was rejected by construction and checked from the per-finger contact time series.

## Common-region gate

The native positive region had 5,000/5,000 initial hand-overlap placements in each group. Two alternative mapper regions with positive opposition/force-closure evidence produced 0/2,048 and 0/1,520 strict index+thumb B-only successes. Including the 512-candidate exploratory mapped region, the mapped search used 4080 candidates with zero strict successes. Together with the 12 native-region candidates, the Phase 2T-R B-only search used 4,092 candidates, within the 4,096 cap.

Because no region simultaneously met dynamic index+thumb graspability, nonzero access in both groups, and absence of systematic A/hand overlap, the B distribution and controller were not frozen. Later stages are not applicable.
