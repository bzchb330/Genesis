# Phase 2R proposal-ready preliminary evidence

**Observed evidence classification:** A: PALMAR_SECURED outperformed FINGERTIP in the paired formal experiment.

The formal experiment completed 4000 trials. FINGERTIP achieved 0/1980 valid-trial successes; PALMAR_SECURED achieved 66/2000. The absolute difference was 3.3000 percentage points, with pair-bootstrap 95% CI [0.011111111111111112, 0.05909090909090909]. McNemar's exact paired p-value was 2.710505431213761e-20.

Digit eligibility was 1/100 for FINGERTIP and 100/100 for PALMAR_SECURED.

These data evaluate directly sampled endpoint states. They do not demonstrate the transfer controller needed to reach a palmar-secured state from a fingertip acquisition state.
